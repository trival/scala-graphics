Scala WebGPU Sketches

Base Triangle

Rotating triangle around Y with a perspective camera. Minimal painter setup with a Mat4 mvp uniform driven by the animate loop.

Rooms — Base

First-person walkable room: an inside-out cuboid textured with procedurally pre-rendered noise (mipmapped). WASD / arrows to move, Space / Shift up·down, drag to look.

Rooms — Canvases

First-person walkable room with plain pre-rendered noise walls (no debug grid), keeping the HDR ceiling halos, bloom and blurred floor reflection. WASD / arrows to move, Space / Shift up·down, drag to look.

Rooms — Columns

Walkable outdoor courtyard of stone columns capped by crossing beams between perimeter walls. One shared shade with per-instance model + normal matrix; a 40-cell grid pattern reveals the face normal at the borders. WASD / arrows · Space / Shift · drag to look.

Rooms — Grid Ceiling

Two crossing grids of strips suspended under a ceiling, each strip-set baked into a single noise texture (rotational simplex), then sampled along grid borders in the room render pass. WASD / arrows · Space / Shift · drag to look.

Post — Bloom

HDR bloom: bright circles glow via a hand-built mip-chain pyramid (threshold → downsample → additive upsample) composited over the scene. Blur radius pulses over time.
